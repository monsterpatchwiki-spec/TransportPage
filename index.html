Princess' Monsterpatch Tools

Team Builder
Plan your dream squad from the ground up, whether you're gearing up for competitive battles or just building a team of favorites. Browse every mon in the dex, dig into their full movesets and passives, and preview offensive and defensive type matchups before you commit. No more guessing what beats what — build smarter, and build the team you actually want to run!
Damage Calculator
Crunch the numbers before you commit to a move. Pick an attacker, a target, and a move to instantly see the minimum and maximum damage it can deal, factoring in stats, types, and passives. Perfect for theorycrafting a competitive set, double-checking a risky nuzlocke play, or just settling an argument about whose mon hits harder.
Breeding helper
Breeding isn't live in Monsterpatch yet, but this tool is already in the works for when it lands. The goal: help you plan out perfect IV lines, egg move combos, and inheritance chains so you can hatch exactly the mon you're after. Still a major work in progress while the breeding mechanics get figured out, so check back as things take shape!
